Snakes and Ladders

Race to square 100. Ladders lift you up, snakes slide you down.

How many players?

How to play

Take turns rolling the die and move your token that many squares. If you land at the bottom of a ladder, climb to the top. If you land on a snake’s head, slide down to its tail. Roll a 6 to go again. You must land exactly on 100 to win, so if your roll is too big you stay where you are.
